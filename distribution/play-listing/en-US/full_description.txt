🐠 Welcome to Pixel Fish Tank - Your Cozy Virtual Pet Adventure! 🐠

Take care of your adorable pixel-art fish in this relaxing Tamagotchi-style virtual pet game. Feed your fish, keep its tank sparkling clean, play fun mini-games, and decorate your aquarium to create the perfect home for your aquatic friend!

✨ KEY FEATURES ✨

🎮 Core Gameplay
• Virtual Fish System - Manage your fish's hunger, cleanliness, happiness, level, and XP
• Unlimited Leveling - Watch your fish grow and level up with no level cap! Each level requires more XP, creating endless progression
• Tank Interaction - Feed your fish and clean its tank to keep it healthy and happy
• Six Engaging Mini-Games - Play Bubble Pop, Timing Bar, Cleanup Rush, Food Drop, Memory Shells, and Fish Follow with multiple difficulty levels
• Fish Color Skins - Unlock new fish colors like Ocean Blue, Coral Pink, Mint Green, and Royal Gold using coins
• Decoration System - Customize your tank with beautiful decorations like plants, rocks, toys, and more
• Daily Tasks - Complete daily tasks to maintain streaks and earn rewards
• Backup & Restore - Export and import your game progress with JSON validation

📱 Home Screen Widgets
• Small Fish Status Widget (2x1) - Quick view of your fish's mood, hunger, level & coins
• Medium Fish Status Widget (3x2) - Detailed view with all stat progress bars
• Streak Counter Widget (2x2) - Track your daily streak and best streak record
• Daily Tasks Widget (4x3) - Task checklist with completion status and dynamic messages
• Beautiful pastel gradient backgrounds with color-coded progress bars
• Real-time updates when game state changes
• All widgets clickable to open the app

🎨 Beautiful Design
• Cute pixel art with chibi-style fish
• Cozy aquarium atmosphere with soft pastel colors
• Smooth animations for a relaxing experience
• Material Design 3 theming

💾 Offline-First
